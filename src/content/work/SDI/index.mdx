## Context

Building on decades of community-led public health data collection in informal settlements, this project co-designs healthier urban environments alongside local residents and NGOs. By translating complex data into accessible visual tools, it equips communities to influence policy-level decision-making.

## What I do

I support the data visualization and spatial mapping of key aspects of the dataset.

This has included:
-Data cleaning and schema-normalization of community datasets in Python, standardized multi-year data
-Applied regression modeling to isolate primary indicators of community inequality, identifying key variables driving local health disparities
-Integrated quantitative survey metrics with geospatial layers in ArcGIS, producing interactive thematic maps to pinpoint high-priority intervention zones
-Translated complex quantitative findings into executive summaries for community partners, refining survey methodologies to capture emerging health indicators
