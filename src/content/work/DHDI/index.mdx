## Context

The Digital Health Disparities Initiative (DHDI) aims to empower smallholder farmers and pregnant mothers in the DRC to understand how climate change and environmental/climate events impact their crops, food and lives.

## What I did

I primarily worked on communication between the UC Berkeley team and DRC team, as well as creating the backend flow of the forms and tools needed for data collection for the NGO on the ground during their pilot program.

This included:
-Data Infrastructure: Configuring a multi-tiered technical stack featuring KoboToolbox, WhatsApp and Google Workspace across multiple field stations.
-Technical Support & Operations: Coordinating live pilot deployment, providing responsive troubleshooting to field teams to eliminate data flow friction points and resolve workflow breakdowns.
-Technical Writing: Authoring communication workflow primer and onboarding package with operational channels, technical setup guides and troubleshooting escalation pathways.

## Key decisions

**People First** When emergencies (especially health emergencies) happen, we proritize the people and ensuring that people feel safe and supported to continue the program, even if that means going at a different pace.

**Create Based on Need** Understanding who the community and NGOs are and how they need to be supported was a critical starting point for this project.
