## Context

Healthcare in Action (HIA) is a California-based healthcare nonprofit that seeks to meet people where they are at through street medicine.

## What I did

I shadowed HIA's main data analyst to understand how health data is used to inform and improve the healthcare experience of people experiencing homelessness.

As time progressed, I was able to develop insights and analysis on specific prevelant diseases that were common on the streets, in order to aid street medicine physicians and care teams.

## Key decisions

**Understand the context** Knowing that the experience of the unhoused population is very specific, extensive secondary research and literature review was taken into consideration before looking at the data.

**Prioritize human experience** Data insights are communicated and compared with real insights and experiences from physicians on the streets.
